# Business Navigator

Satphonix Business Builder V1

Production Specification

Company: Satphonix Business Development
Product: Satphonix Business Builder
Version: V1
Primary purpose: Turn website discovery, sales, pricing, payment and onboarding into one personalized self-service experience for non-technical business owners.

1. Product Vision

Satphonix Business Builder is not a conventional website quotation form.

It is an interactive digital business diagnostic and configuration platform that helps a prospect understand what their business actually needs from a website and related digital systems.

The central promise is:

Tell us about your business. We'll show you what your digital business needs.

The client should never need to understand:

CRM

APIs

databases

automation

programmatic SEO

schema

integrations

AI agents

webhooks

technical architecture

They describe their business, goals and problems.

Satphonix translates those answers into technology, recommendations and pricing.

2. Core Product Philosophy

Rule 1 — Sell outcomes, not technology

Never ask:

“Do you need a CRM?”

Ask:

“What happens when someone contacts your business?”

Never ask:

“Do you need programmatic SEO?”

Ask:

“Would you like your business to appear when people search Google for the services you provide in different locations?”

Never ask:

“Do you need an API integration?”

Ask:

“Do you currently use other tools that your website needs to work with?”

Rule 2 — Personalize early

The system should collect:

First name

Business name

Business description

Business type/industry

City/location

Service area

Primary business goal

These details should personalize the experience throughout the diagnostic.

Example:

“Let's see what Jones & Jones Solicitors could become.”

rather than:

“Let's configure your website.”

Rule 3 — One engine, many industries

The platform should not require separate applications for different industries.

The core engine remains the same.

The following change dynamically:

terminology

examples

questions

scenarios

recommendations

SEO opportunities

conversion goals

content opportunities

The system should be capable of serving:

legal

restaurants

real estate

retail

finance

non-profits

and other legitimate business categories.
